CI Plant — Privacy

Colour Impressions · last updated 11 November 2026

CI Plant is the private production and costing system of Colour Impressions. It is used only by Colour Impressions' own staff, who sign in with accounts the company gives them. It is not offered to the public.

Gmail

An administrator may connect one of the company's own mailboxes to CI Plant. When connected, CI Plant uses Google's Gmail permissions only to:

  • save a quotation reply as a draft in that mailbox, in the customer's own e-mail thread, with the quotation PDF attached (Gmail "compose" permission);
  • read message headers and labels (not message bodies) to place that reply in the right thread and to notice when a person has sent the draft (Gmail "metadata" permission).

CI Plant never sends e-mail: a person opens the draft in Gmail, checks it and sends it. CI Plant does not read the content of e-mails through this connection, does not delete or change mail, and does not share Gmail data with anyone. The connection's token is stored encrypted and can be removed at any time from CI Plant (Disconnect) or from the Google account's security settings.

Other data

Business records in CI Plant (orders, costings, quotations, production) belong to Colour Impressions and are kept in its own database. They are not sold or shared with third parties.
